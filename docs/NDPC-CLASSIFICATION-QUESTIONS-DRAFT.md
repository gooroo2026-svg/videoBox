# Draft inquiry to NDPC: VideoBox registration and data transfers

**Status:** draft only; not sent. Fill in the operator's legal name and contact details, and have a Nigerian privacy lawyer or licensed DPCO review before sending.

**To:** info@ndpc.gov.ng  
**Subject:** Request for guidance on VideoBox DCPMI classification and cross-border data transfers

Dear Nigeria Data Protection Commission,

I am developing VideoBox, a video-sharing service operated from Nigeria. People can create accounts, upload videos for review, view approved videos, create creator profiles, follow creators, comment, save videos, and report content. The service is currently in testing with two accounts. The planned first release is for adults aged 18 or older. Any future access for children would be a separate release. I am considering serving users internationally but have not launched publicly.

I have read the Commission's published DCPMI guidance, which lists public social-media app developers and proprietors under Ultra-High Level (UHL). Please clarify, or direct me to the current applicable guidance, on these questions before public launch:

1. Does VideoBox's described user-generated video and social functionality make it a public social-media app developer/proprietor for this classification? Does that category apply before the service has a large user base or revenue?
2. Which current registration process, category, fee, deadline, and renewal requirements apply? Which current Compliance Audit Return rules and due dates apply to that category?
3. Is a Data Protection Officer, DPIA, privacy audit, or any additional governance step required based on the category and processing? Which items must be handled by a licensed DPCO?
4. The service uses Supabase, but I am confirming its project region and subprocessors. Once I provide the full list of data-processing locations, what transfer mechanism and prior approval, if any, should I document or obtain under the Nigeria Data Protection Act and GAID 2025?
5. Are there amended notices, circulars, or other current requirements that supersede the published guidance I reviewed?

I can provide a product and data-flow description, confirmed hosting locations, and a summary of age restriction and moderation controls. This inquiry does not include user account data, passwords, or service secrets.

Thank you for your guidance.

## Before sending

- Confirm the legal operator identity and add a monitored reply address.
- Confirm Supabase region, web host, email provider, ad/analytics providers, subprocessors, and processing countries; do not guess.
- Ask a Nigerian privacy lawyer or licensed DPCO to review the inquiry and advise on any steps required before launch. Keep NDPC's written response in the launch evidence folder.
- NDPC's FAQ says annual Compliance Audit Returns are due before 31 March, and describes the under-200 exception only for Ordinary-High Level entities, with annual registration renewal. Do not apply that exception unless VideoBox's classification is confirmed as OHL. [NDPC FAQ](https://ndpc.gov.ng/faqs/)
- Published NDPC guidance lists public social-media app developers/proprietors in UHL. This is a serious classification issue for VideoBox, but this draft does not assert a final legal classification.
